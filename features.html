Everything Vistora does right now

Beta release v1.5.0 — what you see here is what works today. We welcome feedback.

Reframe

16:9 to 9:16 automatic reframe

Reframe horizontal footage to vertical in one click. Drag to choose the crop position, keyframe it if the shot moves, then export — no duplicate timeline required.

Shown: the live 9:16 guide over a horizontal clip on the Program monitor.
Caption-Based Editing

Edit from the transcript

Transcribe a clip, then edit by clicking lines of text instead of scrubbing a waveform. Click any line to jump the playhead to that moment. English transcription currently runs at approximately 90% accuracy.

Shown: a timecoded transcript panel with click-to-jump navigation.
Noise Cancellation

Clean up dialogue, locally

Reduce background noise in your audio. Processing runs on your device — nothing is uploaded to enhance a clip.

Shown: the dialogue enhancement panel with before/after comparison.
Titles

Simple titles

Straightforward title templates you can drop onto the canvas and adjust. Not a motion-graphics suite — built for getting text on screen quickly.

Shown: the title panel with a basic template applied.
