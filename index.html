Frequently asked questions

What is Vistora?

Vistora is a local Windows video editor (NLE) for creators. It includes automatic 16:9→9:16 reframe, caption-based editing, noise cancellation, and Fast Export — without uploading your media.

Is Vistora free?

Yes. The Limited Creator Edition is completely free (beta) — no account, no watermark, runs on your computer.

Does Vistora work offline?

Yes. Editing, transcription, dialogue enhance, and export are designed to run locally on your device. An internet connection is only needed to download the app.

Is Vistora good for YouTube Shorts and Reels?

Yes. One-click vertical reframe plus social export presets (Shorts, Instagram Reels/Feed, YouTube, Facebook) with loudness normalize on applicable profiles.
