Core Features

What you get

A straightforward toolset for editing and publishing video.

Automatic vertical reframe

16:9 to 9:16 in one click. Drag to choose the crop, keyframe the position if it moves, export.

Caption-based editing

Transcribe, then edit from text. Click any line to jump to that moment. 90% English accuracy currently.

Noise cancellation

Clean up dialogue and reduce background noise. Works locally on your device.

One-click looks

Vibrant, Cool, Warm, Classic color grades. Fast and consistent.

Basic titles & transitions

Simple templates for titles. A set of transition styles to choose from.

Multi-track timeline

2 video tracks + 2 audio tracks. Screen recording, webcam PiP, voiceover support.

Multiple export formats

FCP/Adobe XML, SRT/TXT subtitles, video snapshots, embedded subs in video.

CPU-only hardware support

No discrete GPU required. Runs on older laptops, ultrabooks, any Windows machine.

Ready to start?

Vistora is free during beta. No account, no watermark, everything local.
