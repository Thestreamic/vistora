FlowDeck Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 August 2026  |  Last updated: 15 August 2026
Privacy summary: FlowDeck is a local-first Windows utility. Your requests are processed by an AI model that runs entirely on your device. FlowDeck does not use cloud AI, does not require an account, and does not include advertising, behavioural analytics, or remote telemetry.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how FlowDeck, a Windows desktop utility distributed through the Microsoft Store, accesses and handles information when you install and use the application. It applies to the release of FlowDeck covered by the effective date above.

2. Publisher and Data Controller

Publisher and data controller: Jaina ("Developer", "we", "us")

Location: Ireland

Privacy and support contact: siteidea6@gmail.com

3. What FlowDeck Collects

  • File metadata and action history. When you ask FlowDeck to do something, it stores a record of the request and the action taken (e.g. file names, paths, what changed) so you have a history and can undo things. This stays on your device.
  • Permission settings. Which capabilities you've granted or revoked (file delete, registry access, etc.), stored locally.
  • Trial and licence state. Locally recorded information about your Free/Pro status, such as first-install date, used to manage the Pro trial period.

None of the above is transmitted to us or to any third party. FlowDeck does not operate a server that receives this data.

4. AI Processing

FlowDeck processes every request using a small AI model that runs entirely on your device. FlowDeck does not use a cloud AI provider. Your request text, file contents, and system data are never transmitted anywhere.

5. Information FlowDeck Does Not Collect or Transmit

FlowDeck does not collect or transmit:

  • Your file contents, documents, or other user-generated content.
  • Your name, email address, account credentials, or contact list. FlowDeck does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Advertising identifiers, behavioural analytics, or usage analytics.
  • Remote crash reports, diagnostic telemetry, or application-performance telemetry.
  • Location data.
  • Payment-card or financial information.

FlowDeck does not sell, rent, or disclose personal information to advertisers and does not display advertising.

6. Permissions and System Access

FlowDeck requests specific, individually-grantable permissions ("capabilities") to read files, delete files, read or control processes, read or modify the Windows Registry, control Windows services, execute generated commands, and create System Restore points. Actions carrying meaningful risk always require your explicit, per-action confirmation before they run.

7. Free Tier, Pro Trial, and Purchases

FlowDeck has a Free tier currently available at no charge, with no time limit and no payment required. New installs also get a 15-day trial of Pro features, which turns on automatically with no credit card required. When the trial ends, Pro features switch off automatically and the app continues running in Free mode.

Pro is not yet available for purchase. When it becomes available, it will be sold through the Microsoft Store, and that purchase — and any payment data — will be handled entirely by Microsoft. The Developer will not process your payment or see or store your card details.

8. Updates and Microsoft Store Services

FlowDeck receives application updates through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may process information independently when you access the Store, acquire FlowDeck, or receive updates. Those activities are governed by Microsoft's own terms and privacy statement.

9. Local Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Data described in Section 3 remains on your device until you delete it yourself, uninstall FlowDeck, or clear it from within FlowDeck's own controls. FlowDeck does not set a server-side retention period because it does not store this information on any server it operates.

10. Security

FlowDeck is designed to minimise data exposure by processing everything locally. You remain responsible for protecting your Windows account, device, and backups. No method of local storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Third-Party Software

FlowDeck incorporates open-source components (Electron, React, SQLite via better-sqlite3, ONNX Runtime, and others), each governed by its own license, packaged for local operation.

12. Children's Privacy

FlowDeck is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. FlowDeck does not require an account and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

13. Your Data-Protection Rights

If you're in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because your data lives on your own device, you can exercise most of these rights yourself directly in the app. For anything else, contact us at siteidea6@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (www.dataprotection.ie).

14. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of FlowDeck after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
