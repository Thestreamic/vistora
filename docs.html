Getting started

Download the latest Windows build from the download page, run the installer, then launch Vistora. On first run you can create a new project or open a recent one from the Project intelligence screen.

Projects & files

Vistora saves projects as portable .vistora.json files. Autosave activates after your first manual save. Media is referenced, not copied — keep source files in place, or use Smart Relink if you move them.

Vertical reframe

Select a clip on Program, open the reframe panel, and drag the live 9:16 guide. Enable manual reframe to keyframe the crop position across a move — the reframe is stored with the clip, so no duplicate vertical sequence is needed.

Script Studio

Transcribe a clip, then click any line to jump to that timecode. Select lines to Mark I/O or make a subclip, and export or import .srt at any point.

AI Dialogue Studio

Mark a quiet, noise-only section of a clip for best results, then choose Enhance Voice. Compare Original and Enhanced playback before committing, and pick a Social, Podcast or Broadcast output target on export.

Frequently asked questions

Does Vistora upload my video to the cloud?
No. Vistora processes video, audio and AI dialogue enhancement locally on your device during normal editing, reframing and export.

Do I need a GPU to run Vistora?
No. Vistora is designed to run on CPU-only hardware, including laptops without a discrete graphics card. Intel i5 8th Gen GPU and later recommended. Dedicated GPU recommended for high-res multi effects, multi-layer workflow.

How do I reframe 16:9 footage to 9:16 for social media?
Open the clip on the Program monitor, enable the live 9:16 guide, and drag it to choose the crop. The reframe can be keyframed and previews instantly without creating a duplicate timeline.

Is Vistora free to use?
Yes, Vistora is free to download and use during its public beta.
