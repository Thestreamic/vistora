Latest release

Your phone is now your camera.

This release makes screen recording work properly, lets you use an iPhone or Android as a far better camera and microphone, and removes the judder that showed up when exporting vertical video from phone footage.

What's new

Better recordings, less guesswork.

📱 Use your phone as the camera & mic

One click switches both your camera and microphone to your phone — usually a large step up from a built-in laptop webcam and mic, especially in low light.

Works for screen recording with a camera overlay, and for plain webcam recording. Phones show up in the device lists marked with a 📱 so they're easy to spot.

Nothing is uploaded. Your phone connects over your own USB cable or local network, and the recording is saved to your computer like any other.

🎯 The recorder tells you what's missing

Before, if you hadn't picked a screen yet, the Record button was simply greyed out with no explanation — easily the most confusing moment in the app.

Now it says exactly what's needed in plain language and highlights the button that fixes it. If two things are missing, it points at the first one rather than pulling you in two directions.

🔴 You can see when it's rolling

The Record button turns red and gently pulses while counting down and recording, and reads Starting… then Recording — so the state is obvious at a glance, the way a broadcast deck works.

The recorder panel is also 20% taller, so the whole thing fits without dragging the corner to resize it first.

🖼️ Thumbnails on demand

Right-click any video in your media bin and choose Generate Thumbnail to get a real preview frame instead of a placeholder, with live feedback while it works.

It politely refuses during playback and export, so it can never slow down the work that matters.

Fixed

Including one that stopped screen recording entirely.

Critical

🎥 Screen recording could never start

Every attempt failed with “Recording failed. Please try again.” — a message that told you nothing useful.

What was wrong: unlike a web browser, the desktop app has no built-in screen picker, and Vistora was never answering the request for one. So the recording was rejected before it began. Screen capture now works, including system audio.

🎞️ Judder in vertical exports

Exported 9:16 video from phone footage could stutter during fast pans, even though the original looked perfectly smooth.

What was wrong: phone video usually runs at 29.97 frames per second, but Vistora always exported at exactly 30 — so occasionally a frame got repeated or skipped. Exports now follow the frame rate of your footage. Cropping to 9:16 magnified the effect, which is why it showed up there first.

🪟 Dialogs stretched across the screen

Preferences, Rename Sequence and other pop-ups spanned the entire window instead of sitting neatly in the middle. All of them are back to a sensible size, and Preferences has been redesigned to match the rest of the app.

🧩 Bin thumbnails never appeared

Video clips in the media bin showed a permanent placeholder. Two separate faults were behind it — both fixed — and thumbnails are now generated inside the app from the same video the preview player already uses.

Setting up your phone

One-time setup. After that it's just another camera in the list.

🍏 iPhone or iPad

  1. In Vistora's recorder, click Connect phone…
  2. Pick a helper app (Camo, iVCam or EpocCam) — the button opens the maker's page, where you can scan their code to install it on the phone.
  3. Install it on both the phone and this PC.
  4. Connect with the USB cable, open the app on both, and tap Allow on the phone.
  5. Back in Vistora, press Use phone.

🤖 Android

  1. Windows 11 has this built in — no extra app to buy.
  2. Go to Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Mobile devices and link your phone.
  3. Turn on Use as a connected camera.
  4. Keep the PC and phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  5. Back in Vistora, press Use phone.
If the phone doesn't show up: open the Windows Camera app and switch cameras. If your phone isn't listed there either, the connection or helper app needs attention — Vistora can only see what Windows can see. If it is listed there, close and reopen Vistora's recorder panel to refresh the device list.
